# Research and data sources

1. Mohanty et al. (2016), PlantVillage / image-based plant disease detection.
   https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419/full

2. PlantVillage dataset repository.
   https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset

3. PlantVillage dataset card.
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/mohanty/PlantVillage

4. PlantDoc dataset repository.
   https://github.com/pratikkayal/PlantDoc-Dataset

5. PlantDoc standardized/object-detection documentation.
   https://github.com/ai-agriculture-circuits-and-systems/plant_doc_detection

6. TensorFlow transfer learning tutorial.
   https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials/images/transfer_learning

7. Keras MobileNet documentation.
   https://keras.io/api/applications/mobilenet/

8. TensorFlow Lite.
   https://www.tensorflow.org/lite

9. UC IPM — Apple scab.
   https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/apple/apple-scab/

10. University of Minnesota Extension — corn diseases and symptoms.
    https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/crop-production/corn/common-rust-on-corn
    https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/crop-production/corn/northern-corn-leaf-blight

11. University of Minnesota Extension — tomato/potato early and late blight.
    https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/disease-management/early-blight-in-tomato-and-potato
    https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/disease-management/late-blight

12. University of Minnesota Extension — tomato leaf spot diagnosis.
    https://apps.extension.umn.edu/garden/diagnose/plant/vegetable/tomato/leavesspots.html

13. Penn State Extension — scouting and identifying tomato diseases.
    https://extension.psu.edu/scouting-and-identifying-tomato-diseases

14. Government of India, Soil Health Card — FAQ describing tested soil parameters.
    https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/FAQ_Final_English.pdf

15. Government of India, Soil Sampling & Testing Manual — soil organic carbon classes.
    https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/Manual/140723_ManualClass11_12.pdf

16. ICAR — major Indian soil groups and broad associated crops.
    https://www.icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/India_BRICS_Agriculture_Report.pdf

17. ICAR — soil and crop suitability guidance (all-India crop advisories).
    https://www.icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-07/cwr-21-05-2021.pdf

18. Penn State Extension — general agronomic soil pH guidance; crop ranges vary.
    https://extension.psu.edu/soil-acidity-and-aglime

Always verify dataset licenses and source-specific usage terms before redistributing data or publishing a trained model.
